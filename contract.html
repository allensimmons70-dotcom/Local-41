ARTICLE 6

ATTENDANCE

FREQUENTLY USED
MEMBER QUICK GUIDE

Attendance Points

The agreement establishes point values for tardiness, early departures, absences and unreported absences.

Penalty points expire twelve months from the date they are issued.

Employees may also receive point reductions after completing specified periods without receiving additional points.

READ CONTRACT LANGUAGE

An Employee’s attendance control year begins on the day the Employee receives a point and ends 12 months later on the same date.

Penalty points expire 12 months from the date of issue.

Tardiness: 1/2 point.

Check-out early after working less than one-half of the scheduled shift: 1 point.

Check-out early after working one-half or more of the scheduled shift: 1/2 point.

Unreported absence: 2 points.

All other absences: each individual occurrence will incur 1 point.

If an Employee completes 60 calendar days with no points accrued, then 1 point will be dropped from the record.

For each 30 calendar days thereafter with no points accrued, an additional 1 point will be dropped.

ARTICLE 8

JOB ASSIGNMENTS & BIDDING

TRAINING
MEMBER QUICK GUIDE

Training & Qualification

Successful bidders receive the contractual training rate once training begins or within 30 days of accepting the bid, whichever occurs first.

After completing 75% of the required training hours, the employee is to receive a competency assessment.

Once the employee successfully qualifies, the employee becomes eligible for the full rate of the position.

READ CONTRACT LANGUAGE

The successful bidder for a posted job opening will receive fifty percent of the difference in hourly rate for the position once they begin training or within thirty days of accepting the bid, whichever is sooner.

If the employee was not able to move into the new role within thirty days, the employee will be compensated at the training rate until moved into the new position.

After the employee completes seventy-five percent of the training hours they will receive a competency assessment by a training manager or their designee to establish their qualifications.

Upon successful qualification, the employee will be eligible for the full rate of the position.

If the employee is not successful, they will be allotted the additional training provided by Schedule B.

ARTICLE 12

GRIEVANCE PROCEDURE

REPRESENTATION
MEMBER QUICK GUIDE

Protecting Contract Rights

A grievance involves a dispute concerning the interpretation, application or observance of the collective bargaining agreement.

The grievance procedure includes multiple steps, beginning with discussion and written filing when the issue is not resolved.

Members should contact a steward as soon as possible when they believe a contract violation may have occurred.

READ CONTRACT LANGUAGE

A grievance is jointly defined as any controversy, misunderstanding or dispute arising as to the interpretation, application or observance of any provision of the Agreement.

First Step grievances that are not resolved are to be reduced to writing within fourteen calendar days of the violation.

Grievances not resolved at Step 1 are submitted to the CertainTeed / Teamsters Local 41 Grievance Committee at Step 2.

If the dispute remains unresolved, the agreement provides a process for arbitration through FMCS.

ARTICLE 13

DISCHARGE & SUSPENSION

DISCIPLINE
MEMBER QUICK GUIDE

Discipline & Representation

Article 13 contains timing requirements for company discipline and discharge.

The Article specifically states that these provisions do not apply to discipline issued under Article 6, Attendance.

When disciplinary action is being taken or considered, a Steward is to be present whether the employee requested one or not.

READ CONTRACT LANGUAGE

These provisions do not apply to discipline under Article 6 — Attendance.

Discharge and discipline must be taken by the Company within fourteen calendar days of the incident or knowledge of the incident.

If more time is required, the Company must provide written notification of the need for additional investigation time to the Local Union within the original fourteen calendar days.

Any time disciplinary action is being taken or considered with an Employee, a Steward will be present regardless of whether requested by the Employee or not.

ARTICLE 18

OVERTIME

WORK HOURS
MEMBER QUICK GUIDE

Overtime Distribution

The agreement provides that overtime should be distributed as equally as possible among employees of the same classification.

The Company is required to maintain accurate records of overtime offered.

The Company and Union may periodically review those overtime records.

READ CONTRACT LANGUAGE

Hours of overtime will be distributed as equally as possible among Employees of the same classification.

Accurate records of overtime offered will be kept.

The Company and the Union will hold meetings periodically at the request of either party to review the overtime record.

Equalization is limited to assigning priority for future overtime when overtime becomes available.
